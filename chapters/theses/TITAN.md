**Original thesis (as submitted):**
> Rising per-capita GDP and an expanding middle class in India are shifting spending
> toward branded, discretionary goods. Titan Company (India: TITAN) — India's largest
> branded jewelry and watch retailer — is positioned to benefit because branded
> jewelry/watches are a classic "first discretionary upgrade" category as households
> cross from survival to lifestyle spending.

## Human Judgment Notes (NEEDS HUMAN)

- **Claim 3a is the load-bearing gap in this thesis.** The macro data (claims 1–3) and the company data (claims 5–6) are both individually solid, but the *connective claim* — that general durable-asset spending growth translates into branded jewelry/watch spending specifically — is not established by any source found. This is the analytical leap the thesis maker (not the AI, not the source) is choosing to make.
- **Gold price sensitivity is a live, material risk** to the "positioned to benefit" claim (#6) that the original one-sentence thesis did not mention. Titan's quarter-to-quarter results have swung meaningfully based on gold price moves alone, independent of any structural middle-class trend.
- **The middle-class growth figure (claim 2)** is a secondhand citation of an OECD projection via a government minister's speech — directionally credible but not the primary source. Treat as strong-but-not-audit-grade evidence.
