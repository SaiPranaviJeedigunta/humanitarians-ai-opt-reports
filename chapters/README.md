# Your Own Mycroft — Investment Research Desk

An investment research desk for auditing investment theses claim by claim,
tagging each claim as verified, taste, or inferred, with sources cited where
they exist. The current thesis under audit is Titan Company (India: TITAN),
owned by Pranavi. CANNOT-KNOW.md records the boundaries of this process —
time horizon, exit conditions, the defend-it test, and position sizing —
areas that remain human judgment calls, not sourced facts. This desk performs
read-only research and analysis; it does not decide or place trades.
